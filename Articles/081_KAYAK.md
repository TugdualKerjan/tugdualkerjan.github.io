# KAYAK THE LAKE
![](kayakguy.png)

I was bored and looking at FB marketplace, and ended up buying two kayaks with a friend - taking city bikes one way, bringing them back in 8 C water and nearly catching pneumonia after having both fallen over multiple times, water damaging my phone in the process. This was the start of a wonderful adventure, and most recently with a childhood friend Laurent we crossed the lake Léman and back, totaling 26 km over 4h40 back and forth - with a quick bite at Evian les Bains and a croissant, taking a break at the halfway point to cross the border swimming. These bad boys have seen a lot of action - great money spent, considering that they can probably be sold off for the same price I bought them for !
